# Haunt - Product Overview

## What is Haunt?

Haunt sends three synthetic "ghost" users into a website to complete tasks and shows where they got stuck on a spooky dashboard.

## The Three Ghosts

**The Listener**  
Uses only a screen reader's view and the keyboard. Experiences the web through spoken announcements and navigates without a mouse.

**The Phantom**  
Browsing on slow 3G and an old phone. Deals with limited bandwidth, slower processing, and mobile constraints.

**Bebe**  
Uses 200% zoom, reads Punjabi or Hindi, and is wary of tricky wording. Represents users who need larger text, multilingual support, and clear language.

## Hackathon Context

## Ethical Guidelines

**Testing Boundaries:**  
Ghosts only visit our own sites or our own local copies of open-source sites. We never test third-party production sites without permission.

**Respectful Representation:**  
Describe every persona respectfully. These ghosts represent real people with real needs - users who navigate with screen readers, face bandwidth constraints, or require accessibility accommodations.

**Test Integrity:**  
Never weaken or delete a test to make it pass. If a ghost gets stuck, that's valuable information about a real accessibility barrier. The goal is to find problems, not to hide them.
